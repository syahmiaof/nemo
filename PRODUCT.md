# Nemo
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Developers, freelancers and students tracking projects, commitments, coursework and goals in one browser-local workspace.
## Product Purpose
Track it, finish it. Canonical tasks connect to projects and goals; calendar and insights derive from the same records.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React/TypeScript/Vite app with Dexie, GSAP, backup/restore and offline PWA. No backend, telemetry or accounts. Preserve every existing route and real record. UI remains English.
## Brand Commitments
Original Nemo name and geometric N. User explicitly selected Tavola as the visual and motion benchmark, with no proprietary code/media copying. This sprint follows that supplied direction directly.
## Evidence on Hand
User supplied nine screenshots and the Project Tavola brief; existing source and NEMO_ANTIGRAVITY_MASTER_PROMPT.md.
## Accessibility
Keyboard interaction, visible focus, responsive layouts and reduced motion are required.
